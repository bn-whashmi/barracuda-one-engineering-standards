## Engineering Standards Wrapper

Before generating or updating the feature spec:

- Decide whether the change needs Spec Kit. Use it for substantial features,
  multi-surface work, unclear requirements, or risky refactors.
- Capture the owning issue or tracker link when one exists.
- Keep `spec.md` focused on user value, behavior, edge cases, assumptions, and
  measurable outcomes.
- Do not include secrets, tokens, customer data, private logs, or sensitive
  operational details.
- Mark cross-client parity, security/privacy expectations, and release evidence
  impact when applicable.

After writing the spec, verify that the Engineering Standards Addendum is
complete.
